# Invitation view-family brief

Invitees accept Organization workforce, first-Admin and Platform Administrator invitations through `/organization/invitations/:token`, `/platform/invitations/:token` and `/platform/administrator-invitations/:token`. Invitation authority remains scoped to its server-validated purpose and recipient.

Use approved Plenum branding, one H1, plain recipient/Organization context and one acceptance action. Invalid, expired, consumed, revoked, unauthenticated and unavailable states must offer the appropriate next step without exposing token or internal identifiers. Never imply that viewing the page grants authority. Keep the existing authentication and invitation contracts.

Use a single reading/form region with labeled fields, keyboard focus and visible response. Verify explicit light/dark, 1024x768 and 200% equivalent, reduced motion, forced colors and named desktop AT. PS-842's acceptance ledger owns evidence and unresolved states.
