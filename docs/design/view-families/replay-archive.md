# Replay and archive view-family brief

## Purpose and journeys

Own captured/reconstructed replay, divergence explanation, correction history, legal holds, retention consequences, archive export/import and purge preparation. Prioritize `J-OPR-04` with Reviewer/Admin inspection where authorized.

## Orientation contract

Persist Session, replay source/mode, governing Release, evidence/checkpoint integrity, selected time/event and archive/hold state. Captured versus reconstructed output is unmistakable. Divergence leads with meaning and affected scope; codes and digests are drill-down evidence.

## Layout and scroll ownership

Use a replay frame with timeline/navigation, primary reconstructed surface and evidence inspector. Archive uses a policy/contents summary with consequence panel. Timeline and evidence lists own scroll; the shell and selected time remain stable. Narrow layouts serialize timeline -> surface -> evidence without changing the semantic sequence.

## Forms, dialogs and messages

Replay is read-only. Archive/correction/hold/purge actions show policy, scope, retained/removed material, reversibility and required authority. Mismatch, missing evidence, expired export and failed reimport are explicit. Destructive action confirmation is a final gate after consequence preview, not the first explanation.

The exhaustive family classification is the Replay/archive row of `../forms-dialogs-messages.md`.

## Components and gaps

Reuse timeline, event list, status/integrity badge, difference summary, evidence inspector, policy summary, dialog, sheet, download/import record and inline notice. Gaps: semantic divergence summary and shared evidence inspector with reporting.

## Keyboard and focus

Timeline steps use buttons/list semantics and expose previous/next without requiring a drag scrubber. Playback controls have names/state and respect reduced motion. Evidence drill-down returns focus to the invoking event. Destructive confirmations do not pre-focus the destructive action.

## State, viewport and evidence

The normal family implementation and focused cross-family evidence are closed in `../impeccable/replay-archive/family-closeout.md`; remaining human, device and task-level state gates are explicitly NOT RUN.

All Replay/archive states apply except local pending for read-only replay. Required conditions include no capture, reconstruction pending, integrity verified/failed, divergence, legal hold, archive prepared, partial export, import incompatibility and revoked access. Planned evidence key: `replay-archive`; no PS-800 PASS evidence exists yet.

Tier behavior: 1920/1440 show timeline, replay surface and evidence; 1280x720/600 collapse evidence while retaining time/source/integrity; 1024x768 stacks timeline and surface; 768x1024 and 390x844 use previous/next event navigation plus evidence sheets. At 200%/320px, replay meaning remains available as structured text without relying on spatial animation.
